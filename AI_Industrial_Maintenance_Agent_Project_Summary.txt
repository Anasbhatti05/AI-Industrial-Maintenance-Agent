AI Industrial Maintenance Agent

Project Summary:
The AI Industrial Maintenance Agent is an intelligent maintenance assistant designed to help industrial teams diagnose equipment issues faster and more accurately. It uses AI, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and historical maintenance data to retrieve manuals, SOPs, and previous issue records, then recommends likely root causes and troubleshooting steps.

Problem it solves:
Industrial plants often store maintenance information across many files and systems, making it difficult for technicians to find the right guidance quickly. This leads to longer downtime, higher costs, and slower repairs.

How it works:
1. The technician enters a maintenance issue or machine symptom.
2. The system searches uploaded manuals, SOPs, and historical records.
3. It identifies the most relevant documents and similar past cases.
4. The AI agent suggests probable root causes and repair steps.
5. It generates a technician-friendly summary and recommendation report.

Why this is valuable:
It reduces downtime, improves troubleshooting efficiency, and helps teams work with faster, more informed decisions.

Technology stack:
- Frontend: Streamlit or a simple web app
- Backend: FastAPI
- LLM: OpenAI / Claude / Gemini
- RAG: FAISS or Chroma
- Document processing: PDF parsing and chunking
- Deployment: Render, Railway, Docker, or a cloud VM

MVP scope:
- Upload manuals and SOPs
- Enter machine fault description
- Retrieve relevant documents
- Show probable root causes
- Recommend next actions and checklist

Final statement:
This project combines AI and real industrial maintenance workflows to create a practical and valuable business solution.
